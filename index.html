How to play

  1. Every tree gets one tent right next to it (up, down, left or right, not corners).
  2. Tents never touch each other, not even corner to corner.
  3. The numbers show how many tents are in each row and column.

Tap a square once for grass (a dot: "no tent here"), twice for a tent, three times to clear it. Stuck? The Hint button explains one step.
